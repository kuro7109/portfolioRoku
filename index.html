About Rohit Kumawat

Designer by craft, product thinker by habit

I'm Rohit, a product designer and a product manager based in Delhi.

My interest in design started with xyz (the moment it clicked for me). At IIT Delhi it found a home in Design Club, where I grew into Vice President and later joined its advisory panel.

Those roles opened doors beyond campus: brand and web work with Genme and TickAI, which eventually led me to Aspora and Evvolv.ai.

I care about purpose-driven brands and interfaces: start from what people actually need, try a lot of things, and ship what serves both the business and the person using it.
