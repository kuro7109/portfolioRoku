About Rohit Kumawat

Designer by craft, product thinker by habit

I'm Rohit, a product designer and product manager based in Delhi.

I designed interfaces and user experiences at Aspora in Bengaluru, and planned product and go-to-market work at Evvolv.ai in New Delhi. Outside internships I lead projects with Design Club IITD and take on brand and web work for startups, from Genme's identity to TickAI's website.

I care about purpose-driven brands and interfaces: start from what people actually need, try a lot of things, and ship what serves both the business and the person using it.
